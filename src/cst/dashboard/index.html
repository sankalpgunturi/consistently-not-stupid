        Our strategy
        • High confidence: target outcomes above 90%.
        • Short horizon: aim to enter near resolution.
        • Small stakes: spread roughly $1 bets across independent outcomes.
        • Protect capital: count fees before considering profit.

        The thesis we are testing. Zero loss is the goal, not a guarantee. Timing rules are still under review; the runner currently requires at least two hours to close.
