# Gaps

Choices filled in so the desk could run. Argue with these after the flight. None of them are a promise that the book makes money.

- **The entry record is the price with time still left, not a bootstrap of our own clips.** Each scan reads settled Kalshi markets and keeps the latest trade at least `min_hours_to_expiry` (default two hours) before close, when that price is still a favorite from 90¢ up to but not including 0 or 1. The favorite won if `result` matches that side. A final print is the price of a market about to settle, so it is not a sample. A market with no trade that far before close is checked and left out of the counts. Each scan looks up at most 40 of those trades; the rest wait for a later scan, so the record can stay thin until enough scans have run. A ticker this desk has settled is counted once, from our own settlement. The Wilson bound still has to clear the ask, the fee, and the minimum edge, and a bucket under 30 observations cannot buy. We do not buy unconfirmed clips to manufacture a sample. The governor's profit window is only this desk's own settlements, not the venue seed.
- **Live sending does not exist.** There is no arm button and no order POST. Adding signed orders, acknowledgements, reconciliation, and a kill path is a later reviewed change. Thirty days of paper is an observation window, not evidence the book won.
- **The Decisions drop threshold is 0.6.** A clip is removed only when the matching answer is `drop` and the probability on `drop` is at least 0.6. Below that, or on any bad body, the deterministic set stands. The model cannot add a market.
- **Overrides only tighten, and the floor is 90%.** One step per click, inside the rails. The automatic governor, including a model suggestion, moves one step and only after a settlement that was not already counted. An unchanged book does not ratchet. `min_probability` cannot fall below 0.90. The scan clock is not a risk knob. A tighten that lands while a scan is still reading the book is applied to that scan's fills.
- **No login.** The desk requires a CSRF token. `Origin` and `Host` have to be localhost or the host passed to `--host`, including on the state read and the snapshot socket. An IPv6 bind is the full address, with or without brackets. A wildcard bind such as `0.0.0.0` trusts only loopback names. That is not an account system.
- **Kalshi fees use the standard 0.07 schedule, rounded up to the next cent.** Some series use another multiplier. It is not in the public market payload, so those fills may overstate or understate the cut.
- **The scan is not the whole venue.** It is the first pages of open Kalshi markets, and event titles are fetched only for high bids, capped. The funnel says so.
- **A stop can book a small loss.** If the bid falls 8¢ under the entry and the bid has size, the paper clip sells. Marks themselves use the bid minus the exit fee, so a new buy usually shows a dip equal to the spread and the fee.
- **Entries are slower than marks.** New buys are considered every 10 minutes. Open clips are marked about once a minute. A quote can move between scans.
- **Settlement waits for Kalshi `result`.** A clip settles when `result` is `yes` or `no`. A price near 0 or 1 does not settle it. If the flag is missing, the position stays open. A settled payload that omits the bid and the ask still settles from the winner.
- **A person cannot force a buy** the fee rule, the horizon, the depth check, or the settled record refused.
- **Paper size is the displayed touch.** Kalshi publishes bids, so the other side's bids are the ask. An unfamiliar payload is a refusal, not a fill.
